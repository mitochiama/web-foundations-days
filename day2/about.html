About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for jotting down ideas the moment they occur to you. It keeps your notes in one place so nothing gets forgotten.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the note box on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Read your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add notes quickly
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Works in any modern browser
